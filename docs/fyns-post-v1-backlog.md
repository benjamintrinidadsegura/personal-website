# FYNS post-V1 backlog

FYNS V1 was accepted with Self, Career, Problem, Idea, and the Context Scenes figure system. The items below are intentionally deferred. They are not V1 defects and must not be implemented as part of the V1 checkpoint.

## Product principle

Preserve: **Menschen in Situationen, nicht Menschen als Typen.**

FYNS must retain human interpretive authority, revisability, multiple possible directions, privacy, and appropriate professional and safety boundaries. Context Scene figures remain companions within situations; they must not receive personality assignments, character types, percentages, or user identity mappings.

## Problem: deeper solution layer

A future Problem iteration may extend situation clarification with multiple contextual action directions. Each direction should explain why it may fit, its trade-offs, what could make it inappropriate, and the smallest reversible experiment.

Possible direction families include direct influence, conversation or negotiation, information gathering, boundary-setting, external support, and adapting to constraints that cannot be controlled. FYNS must continue to present options rather than claim one correct solution.

## Idea: deeper implementation and action layer

A future Idea iteration may add implementation paths, validation approaches, concrete experiments, existing-solution or competitor discovery, research suggestions, relevant expertise, technical or documentation resources, organizations, communities, useful external tools, and actionable next steps.

The current working-card and learning-experiment foundation remains authoritative for V1. Future additions must not imply that an idea, market, demand, feasibility, or outcome has been validated when it has not.

## FYNS Action Layer — From Insight to Action

The shared long-term direction may connect FYNS results to concrete, contextually relevant resources, including verified websites, official resources, organizations, documentation, suitable communities, verified contact channels, phone numbers where appropriate and current, relevant experts or people, Digital HQ People discovery, interviews, Partners, and deliberately Recommended people.

External and contact information must be current and verified when surfaced. Never fabricate people, phone numbers, websites, professional resources, or contact information. Preserve transparent provenance and distinguish clearly between Interviewed, Partner, Worked with, and Recommended.

The separate Digital HQ People / Interview / Partner / Advertising Partner World Map remains outside FYNS.
