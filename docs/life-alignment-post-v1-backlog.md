# Life Alignment post-V1 backlog

Life Alignment Representative Modular V1 is intentionally local-only and qualitative. Self, Partner / Relationship, and Life Vision define the functional V1 family. Their V1 result standard includes visual descriptive summaries, evidence-linked cross-signal synthesis, concrete examples, internal paths, small tools, and closing orientation. The items below are explicitly outside V1 and must not be treated as implied follow-up work.

## Binding future modules

The following modules remain part of the Life Alignment product vision, but their functional journeys, result models, visualizations, and module-specific safety treatment are post-V1:

- Family
- Friendship
- Career — focused on how work fits into a broader desired life, not a duplicate of FYNS Career
- Team
- Founder

The V1 hub may name these modules with an honest unavailable state. It must not provide fake or empty journey routes for them.

- A life score, trend score, aggregate benchmark, ranking, or predictive model.
- Personalized medical, psychological, legal, financial, employment, housing, or relationship resources.

## Binding product direction for deeper results

### Further depth beyond the V1 result layer

V1 already requires qualitative, evidence-linked cross-signal synthesis and multiple concrete internal paths for Self, Partner, and Life Vision. Future work may deepen longitudinal or cross-session interpretation only after an explicit persistence and product review. It must remain traceable to explicit input, revisable, and free from pseudo-psychological certainty or hidden scoring.

### Human Context Action Layer

Life Alignment should participate in the future Human Context Action Layer already identified for FYNS. Potential resources include verified websites, official information, tools, organizations, communities, relevant experts or people, Digital HQ People, Interviews, Partners, deliberately Recommended people, verified contact channels, and current phone numbers where appropriate. External and contact information must be current and verified. Resources or contact information must never be fabricated.

### Stronger qualitative visualization

Future exploration may include a richer qualitative landscape, capacity or support flows, current-versus-desired direction views, tension and constraint relationships, or a life-area constellation or other spatial representation. The goal is: “Understand the shape of my current life at a glance,” not “Measure how good my life is.”

Hard constraints remain binding:

- No aggregate Life Score or percentage alignment.
- No pseudo-scientific coordinates or ranking of a person’s life.
- No red/green good/bad simplification.
- No hidden numerical model presented as truth.
- Every visual signal must remain explainable and accessible.
- Text remains authoritative.

Advanced spatial or constellation visualizations, capacity/support flows, and richer Self graphics remain post-V1. The accessible Partner Comparison Landscape and Life Vision Future Direction Landscape are required V1 product surfaces.

## Shared product philosophy

The architectural principle `INSIGHT → UNDERSTANDING → ACTION` may inform future Human Context work. FYNS V1 remains accepted and must not be reopened as part of Life Alignment; any FYNS depth changes require their own post-V1 review.

## Persistence gate

Any future persistence needs an explicit product and privacy decision first. The design must cover consent, purpose limitation, ownership, row-level access, encryption and operational access, retention, backups, breach impact, export, deletion, account separation, and cross-device expectations. It must use a new additive data model and must not reuse comment identity/profile tables.

## Research and accessibility follow-up

- Test whether the starter areas and custom-label limits work across different life situations and cultures.
- Validate the qualitative signal language without turning tension into failure or change into an obligation.
- Run moderated keyboard, screen-reader, mobile reflow, zoom, reduced-motion, and multi-page print sessions.
- Revisit discovery phrasing so Life Alignment remains distinct from FYNS Self.
